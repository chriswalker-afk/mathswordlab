How to play Head to Head

1
Choose your modePlay word → definition or definition → word, then enter the two player names.
2
Take turnsOnly the active player answers. Their own clock runs down while they think.
3
Win the duelA correct answer scores 1 point and passes the turn. A wrong answer stays on the same question until they get it right or pass.
Match rules
Correct answer: +1 point
Pass: turn ends and 2 seconds are removed from that player's clock
Wrong answer: no point, but the clock keeps running

When both clocks reach zero, the player with the most points wins.
